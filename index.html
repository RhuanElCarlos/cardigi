  • A small number of Royal Knights may sometimes conscript the Digimon in their environs to help accomplish their mission. Their methods in recruiting allies depends upon the mission at hand, but Craniamon will obtain allies by challenging a Digimon it recognizes as strong to a duel and forcing it to submit. This mounted form is adopted by Craniamon during a mission after defeating Enbarrmon and commandeering it to use as their horse. Always a perfectionist, it is believed that Craniamon set its eyes on Enbarrmon's high mobility in order to more efficiently accomplish its tasks. While they have fought against each other in many a fierce battle, recognizing their respective strengths and forging a strong bond of trust with one another has enabled them to develop astonishing levels of mobility and offensive prowess. With their Ruthless Charge special move, Enbarrmon dashes at full speed for a piercing attack with the Claíomh Solais lance. Enbarrmon's lightning-fast thrust uses the masses of both Digimon, and with Craniamon's brute strength behind it, most other Digimon hit by the attack will be obliterated into nothingness. In addition, the "never die" protection that Enbarrmon grants to those who ride it affects Craniamon's magical shield Avalon. While mounted, the three-second time limit of the omni-directional Breath of the Gods defensive maneuver is removed, and all attacks are permanently nullified
